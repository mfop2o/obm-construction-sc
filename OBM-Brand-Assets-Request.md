# Request for OBM Construction — Official Brand Assets & Real Project Content

**Prepared by:** Web Development Team
**For:** OBM Construction S.C. (Oromia Building & Machinery Construction)
**Purpose:** This document lists every real asset, brand element, and piece of content we need from OBM Construction to go live with an authentic, accurate website. Since launch we have used clearly-marked **placeholder** photos, names, figures, and narratives so the design and layout could be developed. None of that placeholder content represents OBM's real work. To publish, we need the real items below.

---

## 1. Why We Need This

Our current site — public website **plus** the private admin dashboard — is fully built and working in **three languages (English, Amharic, Oromo)**. Everything technical is ready. What is missing is *your* real identity and *your* real work:

- Your real **motto / tagline / brand statement** (currently we use neutral placeholder phrasing).
- Your real **logo** in every format the site uses.
- Your real **project list** with real names, locations, sizes, clients, and photos.
- Your real **services**, **team**, **testimonials**, **figures/stats**, **career openings**, and **office/contact details**.

Supplying these is the only step standing between "working template" and "live OBM website."

## 5. How to Send the Assets

Easiest options (any one is fine):
1. **Shared folder** (Google Drive / Dropbox / OneDrive) with subfolders: `logo`, `hero`, `projects`, `services`, `team`, `testimonials`, `about`, `documents`.
2. **ZIP file** emailed with the same structure.
3. **Copy over to us live** — walk us through each and we capture what's needed.

**Preferred formats:**
- Logos: **SVG** (best) or **PNG** with transparent background, at 2× size
- Photos: **JPEG/WebP**, minimum 1024px wide, ideally 1600px+
- Documents (certifications, registration): PDF or high-res scan

> If any item is genuinely unavailable or not needed, just mark it "N/A" on a copy of this list — defaulting template content is always better than publishing *wrong* OBM content.
